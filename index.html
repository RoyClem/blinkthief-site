Blink Thief

DON'T. BLINK.

Blink Thief is a game for iPhone where gremlins steal your treasure, but they can only move while you blink. Your phone's front camera watches your eyes. Stare them down, bonk them, and wink to fire the Stink Eye.

Support

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/RoyClem/blinkthief-site/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The game says to look at the screen and won't start.
Blink Thief needs camera access. Go to Settings → Blink Thief and turn on Camera. Hold the phone at arm's length in good light, with your whole face in view.

My blinks or winks aren't detected.
Face ID iPhones track eyes best. Make sure your face is well lit and not backlit, and remove sunglasses.

Face tricks (Roar, Slow-mo, Cheek puff, Gust) don't appear.
They need an iPhone with Face ID. Gust also needs microphone access: Settings → Blink Thief → Microphone.

How do I duel a friend?
Tap Live Duel → Invite by text. Your friend opens Blink Thief, taps Live Duel, enters the code and taps Join. Both phones need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center).

My scores aren't on the leaderboard.
Sign in to Game Center in Settings. New scores can take a few minutes to appear.
