Privacy Policy

BLINK THIEF

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version: Blink Thief does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The camera and microphone are used only while you play, only on your device, and nothing is recorded.

Camera and face tracking

Blink Thief uses the front camera to see when you blink, wink, or look away. On iPhones with Face ID it uses Apple's ARKit face tracking (including the TrueDepth camera) to read facial expressions such as eye blinks, an open mouth, raised eyebrows, puffed cheeks, and puckered lips. On other iPhones it uses Apple's Vision framework to find your eyes in the camera image.

  • Face data is processed in real time, on your device, only to control the game.
  • No images, video, or face data are recorded, stored, or sent anywhere, and none of it is shared with us or any third party.
  • Face data is not used for advertising, marketing, identification, or any purpose other than gameplay.

The "Caught you blinking" replay is made from pictures of the game screen (the gremlins and your treasure), not from the camera. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Microphone

If you allow it, Blink Thief listens to the microphone during a game for the "Gust" trick (blowing on your phone). It only measures how loud and low the sound is, a few times per second. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Game Center

Leaderboards and Live Duels use Apple's Game Center. When you are signed in, Apple handles your Game Center nickname and scores under Apple's Privacy Policy. During a Live Duel, your game status (score, treasure left, blink count, and whether your eyes are closed) is sent to your opponent's phone through Game Center. No camera images or audio are ever sent.

Text invites

If you tap "Invite by text," Blink Thief opens Apple's Messages screen with a duel code. You choose who to send it to. Blink Thief does not read your contacts or messages.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, badges, Daily Challenge streak, and settings are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

Blink Thief has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no sign-up. It does not collect any data that identifies you.

Children

Blink Thief does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
